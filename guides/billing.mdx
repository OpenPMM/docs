OpenPMM charges for each Workspace. Account members do not add a charge.

The monthly price is USD 9.95 or EUR 9.95 for each Workspace. The annual price is USD 99.50 or EUR 99.50.

The annual price gives you two months at no charge. Stripe shows the currency and tax before you confirm payment.

## Start the trial

The free trial lasts 14 days. You do not add payment details to start it.

The trial includes all product features except X. Drafts and other destinations remain available.

Open **Account**, then select the monthly or annual trial. Stripe hosts the checkout page.

OpenPMM creates the first Workspace after Stripe confirms the subscription.

## Unlock X during the trial

Open **Account**, then click **Manage plan, payment details, and invoices**. Add a payment method in Stripe.

Return to OpenPMM. Click **End trial and unlock X**.

Stripe charges the first invoice when you end the trial. OpenPMM unlocks X after Stripe confirms payment.

## Use the X allowance

Each paid Workspace includes 50 X post items in each UTC calendar month. Each item in an X thread counts once.

OpenPMM rejects new X publishing when the Workspace reaches 50 items. Drafts remain available.

An uncertain provider result keeps its allowance reservation. This rule prevents an accidental extra provider charge.

## Add or remove a Workspace

OpenPMM increases the subscription quantity before it creates an additional Workspace. Stripe charges the prorated amount immediately.

Workspace removal takes effect at the end of the current paid term. You receive no mid-term credit.

You can use the Workspace until its access end date. The last Workspace removal also schedules subscription cancellation.

Cancel an active Workspace with `POST /v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/cancellation` and a confirmation value of `CANCEL` followed by its exact name. OpenPMM archives the Workspace when the paid term ends. Permanent deletion is a separate `DELETE /v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}` action for an archived Workspace and requires `DELETE` followed by its exact name.

## Handle a failed payment

OpenPMM keeps publishing available for seven days after a failed renewal payment. Use the Stripe portal to correct payment details.

The Account becomes read-only when the grace period ends. You can still view the Account and edit existing drafts.

You cannot schedule or publish new Posts in read-only mode.

## Manage the subscription

Open **Account**, then click **Manage plan, payment details, and invoices**.

Stripe hosts payment methods, invoices, cancellation, and plan changes. A cancellation takes effect at the paid term end.

A change from monthly to annual billing takes effect immediately. Stripe applies the unused monthly amount as a credit on the annual invoice. A change from annual to monthly billing takes effect when the annual term ends.
