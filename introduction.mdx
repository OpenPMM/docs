Open PMM is a product management and metrics platform built around a public REST API. Instead of locking your product data inside a proprietary UI, Open PMM exposes every resource — products, features, metrics, events — as first-class API objects you can read, write, and subscribe to from any HTTP client, language, or agent runtime.

## What Open PMM is

Open PMM combines two historically siloed disciplines — **product management** (organizing and prioritizing what you build) and **metrics tracking** (measuring the impact of what you ship) — into a single, unified API surface. You define your product hierarchy, ingest measurements as they happen, and react to changes through webhooks or the forthcoming CLI. Everything is versioned, authenticated, and designed to integrate with the tools you already use.

## Who it's for

Open PMM is built for three overlapping audiences:

- **Developers** who need a reliable API to embed product and metrics data into internal dashboards, data pipelines, or third-party integrations.
- **Product teams** who want programmable access to their roadmap and KPI data without depending on a vendor's export schedule.
- **Agent builders** who are wiring AI-driven workflows and need a structured, predictable API — and soon a dedicated CLI — to let agents create, query, and react to product signals autonomously.
